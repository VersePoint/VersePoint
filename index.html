About

VersePoint is a free Scripture and worship lyrics projection app designed for churches. It focuses on clarity, simplicity, and distraction-free presentation, helping keep God’s Word at the center of every service.

Features

Scripture Mode

Display Bible verses clearly with high contrast and clean formatting for large screens.

Lyrics Mode

Present worship lyrics with consistent styling and excellent readability.

Built for Worship

Minimal interface designed for churches, volunteers, and media teams.

Platform Support

Available for:
• Windows (PC)
• macOS (Mac)

Free & Open

VersePoint is completely free and open-source. Download the latest version from GitHub and use it in your church without restrictions.
